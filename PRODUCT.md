# Product

## Register

product

## Users

Solo professionals, founders, and independent operators managing personal and professional relationships from a desktop workstation. They operate with high context switches and need immediate recall of who they know, what was last discussed, and who needs attention next.

## Product Purpose

A private, self-owned personal relationship manager that prevents relationships from going cold. It replaces heavyweight sales pipelines with a frictionless system: logging an interaction takes under 10 seconds, and the dashboard answers "who should I reach out to today?" at a glance.

## Brand Personality

- **3-word personality:** Sharp, calm, utilitarian.
- **Voice & Tone:** Direct, quiet confidence, respectful of user focus. No cheerleading, no artificial enthusiasm.
- **Aesthetic References:** Linear, Raycast — keyboard-first speed, purposeful density, crisp interaction states.

## Anti-references

- **Enterprise SaaS bloat:** No deal stages, sales pipelines, conversion funnels, quota indicators, or aggressive KPI cards (Salesforce, HubSpot).
- **Playground / toy aesthetic:** No cartoon mascots, confetti animations, playful doodles, or patronizing gamification.
- **Unstructured spreadsheet fatigue:** Not a raw Airtable or generic database dump without clear relational hierarchy and quick-action workflows.

## Design Principles

1. **Speed over completeness:** Logging an interaction or capturing a note must take seconds, reachable within two keystrokes or clicks. Partial data logged now beats comprehensive data postponed.
2. **Quiet utility:** The UI recedes into the background until summoned. Clear affordances, subtle borders, and intentional density without visual noise.
3. **Calm prioritization:** The interface answers "what demands attention today?" through smart surfacing, not red badges or faux-urgent alerts.
4. **Keyboard-native flow:** Every primary action—from quick search to contact creation and timeline logging—must be fluidly executable via keyboard shortcuts.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG 2.1 AA compliance across all views.
- Strict keyboard navigability with visible, high-contrast focus rings and skip links.
- High-contrast text meeting or exceeding 4.5:1 for body copy and 3:1 for large display elements across both light and dark themes.
- Respect `prefers-reduced-motion` with instant transitions and zero distracting layout shifts.
